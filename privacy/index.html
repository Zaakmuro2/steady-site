Privacy

Your reminders stay on your phone.

Reminder names, times and notes live on your iPhone. If you turn on backup, they are encrypted on your phone before they are sent, and Steady cannot read them.

You sign in with Apple or Google. Steady keeps the account id and email address that service provides, plus the first name, last name and username you choose, so friends can find you and you can sign in on a new phone.

Your conversation with Steady is kept on Steady's server under your account so you can see it on any phone and so Steady can follow up on what you said earlier. Only you and the Steady team can read it. It is not encrypted on your phone the way backups are. You can clear your chat history in Settings at any time.

To understand what you type, say or photograph, Steady sends the message (and, when needed, recent messages and your reminders) to OpenRouter, an AI service, which runs it on an AI model made by DeepSeek. Steady only uses AI providers that do not store your data or train on it. Voice is turned into text on your phone; the audio never leaves it. A photo you pick to read dates from is sent the same way and is not kept. When you ask Steady an everyday question, like how often to change an air filter, the question is also looked up on the web through OpenRouter. You agree to all of this on the screen you see before you start.

Using Steady means the Steady team reads your chats with Steady, and how Steady understood them, to make Steady better. Your messages can include health details such as medication names. Steady also keeps a copy of each request and how it went, under a random id with emails and phone numbers removed, for 90 days. Chats are never used for ads.

Steady counts how the app is used: which screens open, when alarms ring and get answered, and crashes. These counts sit under a random id, with no reminder names and no ads.

If you use Friends, Steady keeps your name, username and friend list on its server. People can find you by name or username unless you turn off "Find me by username" in Settings. When you send a reminder to a friend, its name, times and note are stored on the server, not encrypted, so your friend's phone can receive it. Whether you complete a reminder is never shared with the sender or anyone else. Blocking someone removes them and stops their reminders. To send notifications about friend requests and reminders, Steady keeps your phone's push notification token and sends through Apple.

If you connect Google Calendar, Steady asks Google for read-only access to your calendar and reads your upcoming events on your phone, so it can ring an alarm before them. Your Google Calendar data stays on your phone: it is never sent to Steady's server or to the AI service, never used for ads or to train AI models, and never sold or shared. If you turn on backup, the alarms Steady makes for your events are included in your encrypted backup, which Steady cannot read. Steady's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google Calendar in Settings in Steady at any time, or remove Steady's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If you join the waitlist, Steady keeps your first name, last name and mobile number so it can text you a code when you are let in. Texts are sent through GoHighLevel, our messaging service. Steady only texts you about your invite, and you can reply STOP at any time.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store (and by Stripe for a few early web subscribers). Steady never sees your card number; it only learns whether your subscription is active and when it renews.

If you get Steady through a creator's link, Branch (a link service) tells the app which creator's invite you came from, so that creator gets credit. Branch sees basic device and network details from that tap and first open. Steady does not use them to track you across other apps, never asks for permission to, and nothing about your reminders goes to Branch.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Friends & privacy > Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, friends, shared reminders, chat history and cloud backup from Steady's server. Reminders saved on your phone stay until you delete the app. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
