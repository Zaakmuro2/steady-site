Privacy

Your reminders stay on your phone.

Reminder names, times and notes live on your iPhone. If you turn on backup, they are encrypted on your phone before they are sent, and Steady cannot read them.

You sign in with Apple, and Steady never receives your name or email. Your conversation with Steady is kept on Steady's server under your account so you can see it on any phone and so Steady can follow up on what you said earlier. Only you and the Steady team can read it. It is not encrypted on your phone the way backups are. When a message needs it, recent messages and your reminders are read by an AI provider that keeps nothing. You can clear your chat history in Settings at any time, and deleting your account deletes it.

Steady counts how the app is used: which screens open, when alarms ring and get answered, and crashes. These counts sit under a random id, with no reminder names and no ads.

Using Steady means the Steady team reads your chats with Steady, and how Steady understood them, to make Steady better. Your messages can include health details such as medication names, and you agree to this use on the screen you see before you start. Steady also keeps a copy of each request and how it went, under a random id with emails and phone numbers removed, for 90 days. Chats are read by an AI provider that keeps nothing, and they are never sold or used for ads. When you ask Steady an everyday question, like how often to change an air filter, the question is also sent to a web search service to find the answer. You can clear your chat history in Settings, and deleting your account deletes it.

If you use Friends, Steady keeps your username, display name and friend list on its server so friends can find you. When you send a reminder to a friend, its name, times and note are stored on the server, not encrypted, so your friend's phone can receive it. Whether you complete a reminder is never shared with the sender or anyone else. Blocking someone removes them and stops their reminders.

If you join the waitlist, Steady keeps your first name, last name and mobile number so it can text you a code when you are let in. Texts are sent through GoHighLevel, our messaging service. Steady only texts you about your invite, never sells your number, and you can reply STOP at any time.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Steady never sees your card number.

If you get Steady through a creator's link, Branch (a link service) tells the app which creator's invite you came from, so that creator gets credit. Branch sees basic device and network details from that tap and first open. Steady does not use them to track you across other apps, never asks for permission to, and nothing about your reminders goes to Branch.
