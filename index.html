Steady

Reminders that ring

The reminder that will not let you forget.

Steady turns a sentence like "metformin at 8 and 8" into alarms that ring through Silent and Focus, and keeps ringing until you answer. Medication, water, movement, the call you keep putting off.

Steady is invite only while a small group tests it. If a friend sent you a link, open it on your iPhone and the code fills itself in.
