Terms

The short version.

Steady is a reminder app. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. Alarms can be delayed or silenced by the phone's own settings, so do not rely on Steady alone for anything where a missed alarm would cause harm.

Steady Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price shown before you subscribe. In the United States it is billed by Stripe, and you can cancel any time from Settings in Steady. Everywhere else it is billed to your Apple ID through the App Store: it renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you manage or cancel it in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings. Either way, you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Using Steady means agreeing to how it learns: the Steady team reads your chats with Steady and counts how the app is used, as the Privacy Policy explains, to make Steady better. Chats can include health details such as medication names. To understand your messages and photos, Steady sends them to an AI service (OpenRouter, running a model made by DeepSeek). If you do not agree, please do not use Steady.

Be kind with Friends. Names, usernames, reminders and notes you send must not be abusive, harassing, hateful, sexual, threatening, spam or otherwise objectionable. There is no tolerance for this. You can block and report anyone from their friend page. Steady reviews reports within 24 hours and removes content and accounts that break these rules.

Invite codes are for people you know. Selling or publishing them may end your access.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Friends & privacy > Delete account. This deletes your profile, friends, shared reminders, chat history and cloud backup. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel that first.

If you join the waitlist, you agree that Steady may text the number you give about your invite. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts.

Purchases through the App Store are also covered by Apple's standard license agreement.
