Terms

The short version.

Steady is a reminder app. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. Alarms can be delayed or silenced by the phone's own settings, so do not rely on Steady alone for anything where a missed alarm would cause harm.

Steady Plus is a subscription billed by Stripe at the price shown when you subscribe. Cancel any time from the link in Settings; you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Invite codes are for people you know. Selling or publishing them may end your access.
